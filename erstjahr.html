Why this exists

Big comparison portals are useful, but Germany's competition authority (Bundeskartellamt) found problems with how they work:

  1. Rankings can be influenced by commissions that providers pay.
  2. Paid ads are sometimes placed above the real results.
  3. Some portals cover less than half of the offers on the market, and you can't see that.

Erstjahr earns nothing from any provider. It shows how every score is calculated, says exactly which providers it covers, and links each price to its source.

Case study

How I built Erstjahr.

A neutral, English-first comparison site for the money decisions international students face in their first year in Germany.

1 · The problem

Moving to Munich for my MSc showed me the problem: international students have to choose a blocked account, health insurance, liability insurance and a bank account within weeks, mostly in German. The big comparison portals help Germans, but they have known weaknesses. Germany's competition authority (Bundeskartellamt) found that rankings can be influenced by commissions, that paid ads can sit above the real results, and that some portals cover less than half of the market without saying so.

2 · The idea

India's Policybazaar showed how powerful one comparison platform can be. I adapted the idea for a different market and user: international students in Germany, in English, with no commission, and with every price linked to its source and date.

3 · Data

I used official sources first: provider pages, the Rundfunkbeitrag site, Deutsche Rentenversicherung and the income tax law. Where providers don't publish prices openly, I used dated independent comparisons and said so. Public health insurance prices are not copied from a table but calculated from the legal formula:

€855 × (10.22% + extra contribution) + care insurance (3.6%, or 4.2% if childless and 23+)

Example: TK (2.69%) → €855 × 12.91% + €35.91 = €146.29 / month. All data is in an Excel file with the formulas included. Download the data (Excel)

4 · A transparent scoring model

Each factor (price, deductible, refund policy, English support…) is scaled so the best option gets 10 and the worst gets 0. The score is a weighted average, and the user sets the weights with sliders. Products that aren't available to the user, such as AOK Bayern outside Bavaria or plans outside their age band, are shown but not ranked.

5 · From prices to decisions

  • First-year cost: sourced prices and the user's own estimates kept visibly separate, compared with the €992 monthly blocked account payout.
  • Cash-flow planner: a month-by-month forecast with and without a job, showing when money runs out and how many hours of work close the gap. It's the same logic as a company cash-flow forecast.
  • Working student calculator: pension contribution and an income tax estimate using the 2026 tax formula (§ 32a EStG), plus the 20-hour rule and the 140-day limit for non-EU students.

6 · How it was built

HTML, CSS and JavaScript in a single page that also works offline, with answers saved only in the user's browser. I built it with AI-assisted coding (Claude). I set the scope and features and decided on the neutral scoring approach, and every figure links to its source so it can be checked.

  • Tracking price changes over time, for example the new extra contributions every January.
  • A tax refund estimator for working students, and a comparison of university cities.

Sources and method

How Erstjahr works.

The score

For every factor, the best value in the table gets 10 and the worst gets 0, with everything in between scaled in a straight line. Yes/no factors give 10 or 0. The final score is the weighted average, using the weights you set with the sliders.

What we cover

What we don't do

  • No commission, affiliate links or paid placements.
  • No star ratings or reviews we can't verify.
  • No selling or brokering. Selling insurance in Germany needs a broker licence (§ 34d GewO); Erstjahr is information only.
Information, not advice. Prices and rules change. Always confirm with the provider or the authority before you sign anything. All prices were last checked on 04.11.2026.
